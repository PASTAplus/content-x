# Self-Publishing

Self-publishing to the EDI data repository requires that you are in the "vetted" user group, meaning that you have the privilege to publish your data package without EDI curator oversight.

[TOC]

## How to become a "vetted" user

When ever you sign in to an EDI service (e.g., Data Portal or ezEML), you are using a unique EDI identifier within our software that is linked to the identity provider you used to sign in (i.e., GitHub, Google, Microsoft, or Orcid, or EDI's account service). For you to become a "vetted" user, we need to add this EDI identifier to a special group designated "vetted" so that our authorization service knows you are permitted to publish data packages directly through our publishing interfaces (see below). To obtain your EDI identifier, follow these steps:

1. Sign in to our [Identity and Access Management service](https://auth.edirepository.org) using your preferred identity provider.
2. From your User Profile page, click on the copy icon &#10697; found at the end of the EDI-ID field just below "Contact."
3. Send your EDI identifier to our [support email](mail:support@edirepositoyr.org) with a request explaining that you would like to self-publish your data package. We will review your request and set up a video meeting to verify that you understand the correct procedures for self-publishing.
